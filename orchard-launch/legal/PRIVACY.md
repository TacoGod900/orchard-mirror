## The short version
Orchard Mirror runs on your PC and talks to your iPhone locally. The mirrored screen, audio, touches,
and anything you paste **stay on your machine and your device** — they are not sent to us. The only
personal data we handle is what's needed to sell you a licence and support you.

## What we collect

**On your computer, not sent to us:**
- The live iPhone screen, audio, and input pass between your PC and your phone locally. We never
  receive, store, or transmit them.
- Your licence key and a first-run setup marker are stored locally in your Windows user profile.

**Sent to us / our processors, only for buying and support:**
- **Payment data** — handled by **Stripe**. We do not see or store your full card number. Stripe
  gives us your email, the product bought, and payment status. See Stripe's privacy policy.
- **Your email and licence key** — to deliver your key, verify your licence, and provide support.
- **Support messages** — if you email us.

## What we do NOT do
- We do not sell or rent your personal data.
- We do not track your activity inside the mirror or on your phone.
- We do not embed advertising or ad trackers.

## Licensing checks
During the beta, licence keys are validated **offline** on your device. If we later add a licensing
server, your key (and a device identifier) may be sent to us to confirm your licence is active; this
policy will be updated before that happens, and this sentence removed once it's live.

## Your rights
Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR in the EU/UK, the Privacy Act in Australia), you may request
access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal data we hold about you. Email [SUPPORT EMAIL].

## Data retention & security
We keep purchase and support records as long as needed for tax, accounting, and support, then delete
them. We use reputable processors (Stripe, [EMAIL PROVIDER]) and take reasonable measures to protect
your data.

## Children
Orchard Mirror is not directed at children under [13/16 — check your jurisdiction] and we do not
knowingly collect their data.
