Questions

Good things to know.

What do I need to run it?

A Windows 11 PC and an iPhone on iOS 17.4 or later. You connect the phone by USB for the one-time setup; after that it can also connect over Wi-Fi on the same network.

Does it work when my phone is locked?

No — Orchard mirrors your unlocked iPhone. It is not a lock-screen bypass and doesn't get around your passcode. It's for using your own phone from your own PC.

Is this an Apple product?

No. Orchard Mirror is independent and not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple. It uses Apple's public CoreDevice developer services to talk to your device.

Why does it need "Developer Mode"?

That's the switch Apple provides that lets a computer stream the screen and send input. Orchard turns it on for you during setup; it's a normal, supported iOS feature.

It's a beta — what does that mean?

It works and it's useful, but it's still being polished, and behavior can change between iPhone models and iOS updates. Beta pricing reflects that. You can cancel anytime.
