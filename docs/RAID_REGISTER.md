# Project Orchard RAID register

Status date: 18 July 2026  
Review cadence: weekly at program level; daily for red items  
Owner: Program Management Office, with named accountable functions below

This is the initial risks, assumptions, issues, and dependencies register for
the production program in [EXECUTION_PLAN.md](EXECUTION_PLAN.md). It is a
living control, not a substitute for the phase gates. Any trigger that crosses
a kill, pause, or pivot threshold in Section 20 of the master plan must be
escalated immediately.

Ratings use probability and impact from 1 (low) to 5 (critical). Exposure is
their product. Residual exposure is reassessed after the listed mitigations
have objective evidence.

Implementation snapshot: strict cross-language IR/profile validation, the Swift
revisioned state engine, and the .NET authenticated current-user transport now
have focused green tests. The native Swift child-to-.NET host process flow is
still under integration, so those controls reduce risk but do not close the
Phase 5 process, sandbox, or promotion gates. The accepted vertical-slice process
decision is [ADR 0002](decisions/0002-native-application-process-and-local-protocol.md);
the bundle/runtime ABI, render-graph, and LSP/DAP decisions remain proposed in
[ADR 0003](decisions/0003-application-bundle-and-runtime-abi.md),
[ADR 0004](decisions/0004-ui-kernel-and-render-graph-boundary.md), and
[ADR 0005](decisions/0005-language-server-and-debug-adapter-boundaries.md).

## Review and escalation procedure

1. Every item has one accountable owner, even when several teams execute the
   mitigation.
2. Owners update probability, impact, trigger evidence, mitigation evidence,
   residual exposure, and next decision date before the weekly review.
3. Exposure 15 or higher is reviewed by the executive program council. Any
   impact-5 item with an active trigger is reviewed within one business day.
4. Security, privacy, legal, clean-room, data-loss, signing, or false-confidence
   triggers can block a release independently of schedule or revenue pressure.
5. Closed items retain their evidence and closure rationale. Risks are never
   deleted to improve reporting.
6. Assumptions that fail become issues or risks immediately. Dependencies that
   miss their required date enter the integrated critical path and reforecast.
7. The GA audit links this register to phase-gate decisions, exceptions,
   incident exercises, test evidence, and the final proposal traceability rows.
