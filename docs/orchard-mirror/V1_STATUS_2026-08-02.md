## Evidence rule

Two states, kept apart on purpose:

- **Verified offline** — a test in the suite, or a measurement taken from a recorded capture, that
  reruns without a phone.
- **Verified on device** — measured against the iPhone, or heard.

Nothing below is claimed as working on the strength of source alone.

## What changed on 2 August

1. **`Lock` now actually locks.** It held Consumer Power for 0.5 s, which leaves the phone awake and
   raises the volume HUD. At 0.05 s it sleeps every time. *Verified on device.*
2. **The Home-swipe recogniser is under test.** It moved out of the `WinExe` to
   `PhoneGestures`, and has six tests written as window fractions rather than as the thresholds
   themselves. Each of its three clauses was deleted in turn to confirm a test fails.
3. **Audio works.** The paired leg is AAC-ELD, not Opus, and the old GStreamer pipeline could
   never have produced sound. It now decodes through libfdk-aac and plays through `waveOut`.
   *Verified on device.* Two things came out of this that outlive the feature: the magic cookie the
   ecosystem publishes for this stream (`F8E64000`, also in ADR 0014 §6) declares the wrong frame
   length, and the decoder choice deviates from ADR §6. Both are in
   [AUDIO_2026-08-02.md](AUDIO_2026-08-02.md).

## What is not done

| Item | Why not |
| --- | --- |
| **Further video smoothing** | The remaining candidates — frame pacing and the queue drop policy — cannot be measured end to end without the device, and the video path is currently working. Speculative changes to it were not worth the risk unattended. |
| **Rounded corners and the resize region** | The corners are 1-bit HRGN clips, so they alias, and the region is rebuilt on every resize event — including on the HWND hosting the flip-model swap chain. Adjacent to the present path, so worth changing on its own against a phone rather than in a batch. |
| **The toast surface** | `StatusRouter` routes routine news to `StatusSurface.Toast`, and nothing draws one yet, so that news is silent rather than small. Silent is already the behaviour worth having; small would be better. |
